Scalability and Density

Ultra HDCi® LX4 high-density fibre patch panels have been engineered to significantly increase density for both patching and splicing — the ideal solution for installations with space constraints.

⇧

Maximum Density

High-density patch panels maximise the amount of adaptor panels per rack unit — ideal where space is at a premium in hyperscale environments.

◑

Split-Top Design

The split top design allows for easier cable management and improved strain relief for the cable ingress — protecting your critical fibre connections.

⇄

Sliding Tray Locking

The sliding tray has locking positions to prevent over-extending fibres — ensuring reliable connections during maintenance activities.

◆

Universal Cassette Mount

LX4 panels have universal mounting hardware to hold fully-terminated cassette modules — maximising networking space while saving installation time.
